# Spotify Widget

**Your music, always in view.**

[Visit the website](https://gwaldron.github.io/spotify-tray/)

A small, always-visible Spotify player for Windows. See the song, artist, and album art, and pause, play, or skip tracks right from your taskbar. Click the artwork or song to bring up Spotify.

## [↓ Download latest version](https://github.com/gwaldron/spotify-tray/releases/latest)

Choose the **Setup .exe** for easy installation, or the **Portable .zip** to run without installing. No administrator access or separate .NET installation needed.

Requires **Windows 10 (version 2004+) or Windows 11, x64**, and the **Spotify desktop app**. Open Spotify and play a song to get started.

- Keep it on the left side of your taskbar, or drag it wherever you like.
- Song details and album art come directly from Spotify through Windows. No screen capture, extra login, or API key required.
- Optional start with Windows keeps your music controls ready.
