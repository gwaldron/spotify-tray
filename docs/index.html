Stay in your flow

Pause, play, or skip a track. Click the artwork or song to bring up Spotify when you want the full app.

Make yourself at home

Keep it on the left side of your taskbar, place it above the taskbar, or drag it to your favorite spot.

Just your local Spotify

Track details and artwork come through Windows’ media interface. No screen capture, extra login, or API key.

Install. Play something. That’s it.

Download and run the installer, then open the widget and play a song in Spotify.
The .NET runtime is included. A portable ZIP is also available.
